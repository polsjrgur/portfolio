SoulTube

Music × Gaming
App / UI·UX Design

SoulTube is an app that combines music and gaming. It records and analyzes users’ listening behavior and translates the results into corresponding visual elements. Using these elements, users can simulate and create their own personal worlds. By building new dimensional spaces and exploring the worlds of others, the project aims to help users better understand themselves and express themselves more openly.

Project Type
Digital Product / App Design
Focus
Music Experience / Gaming / Visual Expression

Music Experience

The music experience records the user’s listening behavior and turns everyday listening into the starting point of a personal visual identity.

From Listening to Visual Elements

Listening data is analyzed through emotional weight, stylistic proportion, and behavioral patterns. These results are translated into visual elements that users can understand and reuse.

Building a Personal World

The generated visual elements become materials for building a personal dimensional space. Each world reflects the user’s listening habits, emotions, and individual preferences.

Exploring and Connecting

Users can enter new spaces, explore the worlds created by others, and discover similarities through visualized compatibility. Music becomes a way to understand, express, and connect.

Listen. Create. Explore.
Build a world that sounds like you.
