MR. WU

小武手办设计

Mr. Wu is a young man deeply influenced by the golden age of Chinese Peking Opera. After an unexpected shift in time transports him into the modern era, he begins exploring contemporary fashion and connecting it with the visual language and traditions of Peking Opera. He documents these encounters in a virtual cosmic museum, serving as an envoy dedicated to preserving and safeguarding human civilization.

Project Type
Character Design / Figurine Design / 3D Art
Character
Wusheng, Chinese Peking Opera
Software
Blender / Adobe Photoshop
Role
Concept Development / Character Design / 3D Modeling / Rendering

Character Design

The character combines the visual identity of a traditional Peking Opera wusheng with the silhouette and functional details of contemporary fashion. Traditional headwear, facial makeup and symbolic colors are reinterpreted through modern clothing, accessories and stylized proportions, creating a figure that exists between cultural memory and an imagined future.

HUA DAN

The Hua Dan character brings the lively movement and ornamental language of the flower-maiden role into a contemporary figurine. The dense red headpiece and stage makeup are paired with relaxed sportswear, headphones and everyday footwear.

Material Variations

MO ROLE

The Mo character translates the composed bearing of an elder male role into a contemporary figure. Traditional headwear, a long beard and precise facial makeup remain central, while modern tailoring and footwear move the character into the project's imagined present.

Material Variations

CHOU ROLE

The Chou character draws on the comic role's graphic facial makeup and alert expression. Contemporary layered clothing and compact proportions preserve the role's wit and immediacy while translating its theatrical identity into a collectible figure.
