Hong Kong lies outside the traditional Coffee Belt, yet recent experiments have shown that coffee can still be cultivated locally. Coffee flavour is shaped not only by variety, but also by origin, climate, humidity, altitude, fermentation and roasting. For Cloud Coffee, the true distinction lies in the environment that nurtures the bean. The project therefore shifts attention from the bean itself to Hong Kong's landscape, climate and spirit of experimentation.

A Brand Shaped by Place

From Bean to Environment

Instead of building the identity around the coffee bean alone, the project shifts attention to the environment that nurtures it. Hong Kong's map, mountainous and coastal terrain, clouds and humidity form the visual foundation of the brand.

Cultivation, fermentation, roasting and preparation are read as one continuous process, allowing each stage to contribute to the character of the final cup.

A testing ground for new flavours.

Identity System

Cloud, Water, Mountain, Coffee

The identity brings together clouds, humidity, mountainous terrain and the coffee drop. These elements describe a product whose character is inseparable from Hong Kong's geography and climate.

Brand Applications

A System in Everyday Use

The identity extends across menus, printed cards, loyalty materials, apparel and campaign imagery, allowing the environmental concept to remain recognisable across different scales and contexts.

Across physical touchpoints, the identity remains quiet and recognisable: a compact system that carries the project from research into everyday use.

Cloud Coffee is more than a coffee brand.
It is an experiment in the possibilities of flavour.

From cultivation and fermentation to roasting, every stage becomes part of an ongoing attempt to discover a flavour shaped by Hong Kong.
